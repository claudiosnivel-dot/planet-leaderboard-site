Planet Leaderboard Uploader

An internal tool of the Planet Leaderboard YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own Shorts.

What it does

Planet Leaderboard publishes short videos that rank U.S. states and countries by official statistics (for example U.S. Census Bureau and World Bank data). The videos are produced on the channel owner's computer.

The uploader uses the YouTube Data API v3 to:

  • upload each finished Short to the Planet Leaderboard channel, as a private video scheduled to go public at a set time;
  • check that it is signed in to the right channel;
  • read back the processing and privacy status of the videos it uploaded.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Planet Leaderboard channel, to publish to that channel. It is not offered to other people, there is no sign-up, and it does not access anyone else's account.
