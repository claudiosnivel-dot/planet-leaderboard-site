Planet Leaderboard Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Planet Leaderboard Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Planet Leaderboard YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. This policy explains what the app does with data from Google.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload, to upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.readonly, to read the channel's identity and the status of the videos the app uploaded.

It does not read comments, subscribers or analytics, and it does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth tokens are stored only on the channel owner's computer, outside any shared or public location. They are never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The app keeps a local record of the videos it uploaded: the video ID, its status and its scheduled publication time.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Planet Leaderboard Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the local token file removes the stored credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Planet Leaderboard channel page.
